An interactive WebGL prototype for exploring packet-level load balancing in Fat Tree, Dragonfly, and Slim Fly style topologies. It visualizes ECMP, OPS, UGAL-L, PLB, REPS, and Spritz variants with live packet motion, queue buildup, ECN/drop feedback, link failures, and a timeline view.
