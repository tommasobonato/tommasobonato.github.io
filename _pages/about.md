    collective communication, and network topologies. I also contribute to the
    <a href="https://ultraethernet.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Ultra Ethernet Consortium (UEC)</a>
    effort, including work on congestion-control and load-balancing proposals and related tooling (mostly 
    <a href="https://github.com/spcl/HTSIM" target="_blank" rel="noopener">HTSIM</a>).
  </p>

  <p>

    <a href="https://www.unibo.it/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener">University of Bologna</a>

    <a href="https://ethz.ch/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ETH Zürich</a>.
    During my studies, I interned at the
    <a href="https://www.esa.int/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">European Space Agency (ESA)</a>

    <a href="https://www.aboutamazon.com/about-us" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Amazon</a>.
  </p>

Outside of research, I enjoy reading about space and space exploration, watching sci-fi movies, skiing,
making (and cooking) pizza, and visiting theme parks.
